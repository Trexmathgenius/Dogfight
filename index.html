Controls
MouseSteer in 3D. Move the cursor away from the screen centre to turn, climb or dive: the further out, the harder. Your plane never stops. A DRoll left / right. Hold to spin; let go and the plane finishes its roll and levels out. Knife-edge makes you a thinner target. Left clickGuns. Rounds refill on their own. Right clickMissile. Keep a target ahead until the bracket turns red (LOCK) and the missile tracks it hard. Heat-seekers give the target no lock warning; semi-active and laser missiles need you to keep the target ahead until impact. Missiles slow down after their motor burns out, so long shots can be outrun. Paired missiles: hold right click to lock several targets, release to fire. WSpeed burst. How long it lasts depends on your plane; the orange bar shows when it's ready again. SHold to slow down and turn tighter. Let go and you speed back up to cruise. FCycle formation: your formation, your saved formations, then presets. PPause

You fly the black plane, your wingmen are blue, bandits are red. Friendly fire is on for everyone: your rounds and missiles can hit your own wingmen, and theirs can hit you. Every kill by you or your squad pays credits.

Erase everything? Credits, gear, wingmen, Endless records, all replays and all three careers will be deleted. This can't be undone.
